Privacy Policy

DailySaathi

Last updated: November 22, 2026

1. Introduction

DailySaathi is a daily routine and household management application. This Privacy Policy explains how information is collected, used, stored and protected when you use the app.

2. Information We Collect

Depending on the features you use, the app may process:

  • Account information, such as your name and email address, when you sign in.
  • Milk records, quantities, rates and monthly summaries.
  • Shopping list items, categories, quantities and purchase details.
  • Shared list information and changes made by authorized participants.
  • App preferences, such as language and reminder settings.

3. How We Use Information

Information is used to:

  • Provide daily tracking and household management features.
  • Save and display your records and reports.
  • Enable account login and shared list functionality.
  • Calculate totals and generate reports.
  • Provide reminders and improve app functionality.

4. Data Storage and Third-Party Services

DailySaathi uses Google Firebase services for supported account authentication and cloud data features. Information required for these features may be processed and stored by Google Firebase according to Google's privacy terms.

Google Sign-In may be used to authenticate your account. Please refer to Google's Privacy Policy: Google Privacy Policy.

5. Shared Shopping Lists

If you use shared shopping lists, information you choose to share may be visible to invited participants. Their permitted actions depend on the access permissions set by the list owner.

6. Notifications

The app may use local notifications and device features to provide reminders. You can manage notification permissions in your device settings and reminder preferences in the app.

7. Data Security

We use the security features provided by the services used by the app and take reasonable steps to protect user information. However, no electronic storage or transmission method is guaranteed to be completely secure.

8. Data Retention and Deletion

Information is retained as needed to provide app features. If you want to request deletion of your account or associated personal information, contact us using the email below. Some information may remain temporarily where required for security, legal or operational reasons.

8A. Account and Data Deletion

DailySaathi users can request deletion of their account and associated personal data. Users can also request deletion of specific data without deleting their account.

To request account or data deletion, email yahkaise2@gmail.com from your registered email address. Include "DailySaathi Account Deletion" or "DailySaathi Data Deletion" in the subject and explain what you want deleted.

Account deletion requests will include deletion of the associated Firebase account and personal data stored in Firebase, as applicable. Locally stored app data will also need to be deleted from the device. Some information may be retained where required by law or for legitimate security purposes.

9. Children's Privacy

DailySaathi is a general household management application and is not specifically designed for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect children's personal information in violation of applicable laws.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when app features or privacy practices change. The latest version will be published on this page.
